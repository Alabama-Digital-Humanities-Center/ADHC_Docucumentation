# Overview
Digitorium is a digital humanities conference hosted by the University of Alabama Libraries, providing an opportunity for researchers, scholars, students, and community partners to talk about relevant topics in the field of Digital Humanities.   

Digitorium started as an annual conference in 2015. In 2020 (during the COVID-19 lockdown), it adopted a virtual format. Since 2021, Digitorium has been a fully hybrid conference with both in person and virtual participants from around the world. In 2024, it became a biennial conference.
